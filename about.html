About Jersey Aficionado

Jersey Aficionado started as a personal collection — football shirts I've gathered over the years, from clubs and eras that mean something to me. These aren't random stock; they're pieces from my own collection that I'm now making available to fellow fans and collectors.

Because each shirt has been part of that collection, every one is described honestly and photographed in detail — with condition and authenticity spelled out on each listing, whether it's brand new with tags or a carefully kept classic.

Shipping

We ship worldwide from the United States. Orders are dispatched within 2 business days via tracked shipping, and you'll get a tracking number as soon as your order is on its way. Shipping is calculated at checkout.

International buyers are responsible for any import duties or taxes charged by their own country.

Returns

Every item is described accurately and photographed in detail — please review the condition notes and (where listed) the measurements before buying, as many pieces are one-of-a-kind. If an item arrives not as described, contact us within 7 days of delivery and we'll make it right with a return or refund.

Authenticity

Every shirt here is an official, manufacturer-made product (Nike, adidas, Puma, and so on) — never a counterfeit. Football shirts simply come in two tiers, and each listing tells you exactly which one you're getting:

  • Fan / replica version — the standard retail shirt most supporters wear.
  • Authentic / player version — the premium, on-pitch spec (lighter, technical fabric).

Both are 100% genuine — the difference is the tier. Premium authentic / player versions are labelled as such; everything else is the standard fan / retail version.
